# kōra — render prompts

Paste-ready prompts for photoreal renders of the space. Written for ChatGPT
(GPT image) but any image model that takes prose will do. They are numbered
in the order to generate them: the overview first, then every other view
with the overview attached as a reference so materials and layout stay
consistent from image to image.

Each prompt is the **scene lock** paragraph plus one **view** paragraph.
Paste both together. Do not shorten the scene lock; it is what keeps ten
renders looking like one café.

Aspect ratios: overview and walls 3:2 landscape; island ends 2:3 portrait;
street elevation 16:9.

The `ō` mark: image models garble it. Leave the mark off every render and
composite `brand/svg/kora-mark.svg` on afterwards (island end, fascia).

---

## Scene lock (prepend to every prompt)

> kōra is a small grab-and-go café in Delhi. The room is a clean rectangle,
> 32 feet wide and 19 feet deep, with a full-height glass front onto the
> street and a 12-by-8-foot island bar in the middle. Materials: pale
> white-oak timber shelving with thin matte-black steel frames, fluted white-oak
> counter fronts, honed pale-limestone counter tops, warm off-white lime-plaster
> walls, pale oak-look porcelain plank floor, one deep forest-green accent
> used only on the fascia, the outdoor chairs and small signs. Signage is black
> capital letters on white backlit panels. Light is warm white, 3000 K, from
> LED strips under every shelf and three opal-glass pendants over the island;
> daylight comes in through the glass front. Everything is tidy and stocked:
> rows of clear bottles of cold-pressed juice in orange, red, green and gold,
> fruit cups, kraft snack bags, white coffee cups. No people unless stated,
> no clutter, no rustic props, no coffee sacks, no exposed brick, no neon,
> no readable text anywhere except the signs named in the view.

## Iterating

| Problem | Change |
|---|---|
| Looks like a game render | add `fine photographic grain, subtle sensor noise, slight lens vignette` |
| Too dark or moody | say `bright, airy, high-key, daylight from the glass front` and cut pendants to one mention |
| Wrong wood | `pale white oak, almost blond, matte finish, no orange tone` |
| Shelves half empty | `every shelf fully stocked in tidy rows, faced forward` |
| Clutter creeps in | restate `no props, no plants except the one named, no hanging signs` |
| Garbled text | drop every word except one sign, or remove signs and add them in Canva |
| Layout drifts between views | attach the overview render and say `match the attached layout exactly` |
