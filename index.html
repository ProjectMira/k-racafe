Our story

Kora is a Tibetan word meaning “circumambulation,” the practice of walking a circular path around. Deeply rooted in our culture, kora is both a spiritual tradition and a familiar routine. It is a moment to move with intention, reflect, and reconnect.

With active lifestyles and busy schedules of their own, our cofounders wanted to create a reliable café for people who are always on the move. They imagined a place where customers could quickly find refreshing drinks, packaged foods, and small pick-me-up snacks at any time of the day.

That idea became kōra, a convenient grab-and-go café designed to make everyday choices simpler. Our thoughtfully selected menu minimizes decision fatigue by offering satisfying options that are easy to choose, easy to carry, and ready when you are. Whether you are commuting to work, heading to the gym, running errands, or moving between appointments, kōra helps you save time without overlooking your daily well-being.

Just as kora is a path people return to, we hope visiting our café becomes a familiar and enjoyable part of our customers’ routines. kōra is a place to pause, recharge, and continue your day feeling refreshed. Our goal is to provide a dependable stop that keeps pace with your lifestyle, one visit at a time.

Our ethos

Short lists

Every drink is built from a handful of ingredients you can name. If it doesn't need to be in the glass, it isn't.

Part of the routine

Not an occasion — a habit. The shot before the gym, the cold brew on the walk to work, the juice on the way home.

Bring your own cup

Carry your own, or bring one of ours back — the discount applies either way. Our packaging is minimal by design, not as an afterthought.

Easy to choose

A tight menu, clearly written. You shouldn't need three minutes and a glossary to order a coffee.
